unsigned short int: suint

Main Structure:

unsigned short int (tile): [0][00][0000000][000][00][0]
Bit 1: Troggy
Bit 2 and 3: Peasant (+ burn statud
Bit 4, 5, 6: Tile has Troghammer, Knight, and/or Archer
Bits 7 - 13: Various Unique Tiles
(13 is tunnel, 12 is mountains, 11 is lake, 10 is forest, 9-7 can be whatever)
Bit 14 and 15: Cottage (+burn status)
Bit 16: Burninated Tile
(tiles are initialized (and modified) in somethiing like board[5][5])
